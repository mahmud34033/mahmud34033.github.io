This dataset contains <b>6,000 manually annotated Bangla Facebook comments</b> representing public sentiment toward the Interim Government of Bangladesh, collected between August 2024 and 2025. The collection period falls immediately following the July 2024 political uprising that led to a major governmental transition in the country.

## Dataset Overview

The central research hypothesis is that Bangla political sentiment expressed on social media can be reliably classified into three categories:
- <b>Positive</b>: Comments expressing approval, trust, or satisfaction toward government actions
- <b>Negative</b>: Comments reflecting criticism, dissatisfaction, or distrust
- <b>Neutral</b>: Comments including factual statements, questions, or ambiguous opinions without clear polarity

## Key Features

- <b>Balanced Dataset</b>: Exactly 2,000 comments per sentiment class
- <b>Data Sources</b>: Major public Facebook pages including Chief Advisor GOB, Jamuna Television, Somoy News, Daily Prothom Alo, Kalbela Digital, and Dhaka Tribune
- <b>Collection Method</b>: Hybrid approach combining manual selection, browser extension-based extraction, and automated scraping via Apify
- <b>Annotation Quality</b>: Three annotators with Bengali language proficiency labeled each comment independently, with an expert reviewer (15 years experience) resolving disagreements
- <b>Inter-annotator Reliability</b>: Fleiss' Kappa κ = 0.91 (almost perfect agreement)

## Use Cases

Researchers may use this dataset for:
- Bangla sentiment analysis
- NLP benchmarking
- Political discourse analysis
- Training or fine-tuning transformer-based models for low-resource Bangla text classification
