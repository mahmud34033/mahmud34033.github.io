This dataset contains **6,000 manually annotated Bangla Facebook comments** representing public sentiment toward the Interim Government of Bangladesh, collected between August 2024 and 2025. The collection period falls immediately following the July 2024 political uprising that led to a major governmental transition in the country.

## Dataset Overview

The central research hypothesis is that Bangla political sentiment expressed on social media can be reliably classified into three categories:
- **Positive**: Comments expressing approval, trust, or satisfaction toward government actions
- **Negative**: Comments reflecting criticism, dissatisfaction, or distrust
- **Neutral**: Comments including factual statements, questions, or ambiguous opinions without clear polarity

## Key Features

- **Balanced Dataset**: Exactly 2,000 comments per sentiment class
- **Data Sources**: Major public Facebook pages including Chief Advisor GOB, Jamuna Television, Somoy News, Daily Prothom Alo, Kalbela Digital, and Dhaka Tribune
- **Collection Method**: Hybrid approach combining manual selection, browser extension-based extraction, and automated scraping via Apify
- **Annotation Quality**: Three annotators with Bengali language proficiency labeled each comment independently, with an expert reviewer (15 years experience) resolving disagreements
- **Inter-annotator Reliability**: Fleiss' Kappa κ = 0.91 (almost perfect agreement)

## File Format

The dataset is provided in two Excel files:

1. **raw_data_bangla_public_sentiment_toward_government.xlsx**: Contains original annotated comments with two columns:
   - Sentence (raw Bangla text)
   - Sentiment (label: Positive, Negative, or Neutral)

2. **preprocessed_data_bangla_public_sentiment_toward_government.xlsx**: Contains three columns:
   - Sentence (original text)
   - Sentiment (label)
   - Clean Sentence (preprocessed text after Unicode normalization, removal of HTML tags, URLs, mentions, hashtags, digits, emojis, non-Bangla characters, and extra whitespace)

## Use Cases

Researchers may use this dataset for:
- Bangla sentiment analysis
- NLP benchmarking
- Political discourse analysis
- Training or fine-tuning transformer-based models for low-resource Bangla text classification
