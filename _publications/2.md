This dataset comprises 6,000 manually annotated Bangla Facebook comments collected between August 2024 and 2025 following the July 2024 political uprising in Bangladesh, designed to evaluate public sentiment toward the Interim Government. The dataset is perfectly balanced with 2,000 comments across three sentiment categories: Positive, Negative, and Neutral. Sourced from major public news outlets and discussion pages, the comments were rigorously annotated by native speakers and validated by an expert (Fleiss' kappa = 0.91). It serves as a reliable resource for Bangla sentiment analysis, political discourse evaluation, and fine-tuning transformer-based NLP models for low-resource text classification.
